# Community expectations

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Critique code and ideas, not people.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and exposing private data are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove abusive content and restrict participation. Send private
concerns to sintaxerror.pvt.ltd@gmail.com. Avoid posting another person's private
information in issues. Reports will be handled as privately as possible; absolute
confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.
